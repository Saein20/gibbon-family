Who We Are

A grassroots network, built by researchers for researchers

GibbonFamily is a global, researcher-led network dedicated to gibbons. We unite scientists working across wild sites with those studying gibbons in zoos and sanctuaries worldwide.

We believe that collaborative, cross-site research is essential for understanding gibbon behaviour, cognition/intelligence, and conservation needs.

Data & Collaboration

Towards a shared gibbon database

We are building the foundations for voluntary data sharing among gibbon researchers. The long-term goal is a community-governed database that enables meta-analyses and cross-species comparisons, covering behaviour, ecology, cognition, and demographics.

Data sharing is always voluntary. We are committed to transparent governance, clear data agreements, and equitable credit for all contributors.
