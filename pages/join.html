Join GibbonFamily

Register Your Interest

GibbonFamily is open to all gibbon researchers including wild and captive, early-career and established. Tell us about yourself and we'll be in touch.

Who should join?

GibbonFamily is for you if…

  • ✓

    You study gibbons in the wild at any field site, on any species

  • ✓

    You work with captive gibbons at a zoo, rescue centre, or sanctuary

  • ✓

    You are a student or postdoc or senior researcher including professors advancing your gibbon research career

  • ✓

    You are interested in gibbon conservation, even if not currently active in research

  • ✓

    You want to contribute to building a connected, collaborative gibbon research community

What happens after you register?

We'll send you a welcome email and add you to the GibbonFamily mailing list. As the network grows, we'll organise virtual meetings, share updates on collaborative projects, and invite you to contribute to shaping GibbonFamily's future direction.
