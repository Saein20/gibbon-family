About GibbonFamily

Our Mission & Vision

GibbonFamily is a grassroots, researcher-led network connecting gibbon scientists across the globe — from rainforest field sites to zoo research departments.

Why GibbonFamily?

The case for a global gibbon network

Gibbons are the most threatened family of primates, with all 20 species facing significant conservation challenges. Yet compared to other ape groups, gibbon research remains fragmented — scattered across field sites and institutions with limited opportunities for coordination and collaboration.

Individual research teams accumulate rich, long-term datasets, but these rarely come together to address broader comparative questions: How does cognition vary across gibbon species? What drives social behaviour differences between monogamous populations? How do ecological pressures shape pair-bond dynamics?

GibbonFamily exists to change this. Inspired by networks like MacaqueNet (macaque researchers), TalCog (great ape cognition), and ManyPrimates, we believe a connected gibbon research community can achieve what individual studies cannot.

Core Principles

How we operate

GibbonFamily is built on principles of openness, equity, and researcher autonomy. Participation is always voluntary, and all members retain full ownership of their data.

  • Voluntary participation: Researchers join, share, and collaborate entirely on their own terms.
  • Equitable credit: All contributors to shared datasets and collaborative analyses receive appropriate recognition and authorship.
  • Open science: We encourage transparent methods, pre-registration, and open publication wherever possible.
  • Inclusive: GibbonFamily welcomes researchers at all career stages, from all countries and institutional settings.
  • Conservation-linked: Our research aims support the conservation of gibbons and their habitats.
