About GibbonFamily

Our Mission & Vision

GibbonFamily is a grassroots, researcher-led network connecting gibbon scientists across the globe from rainforest field sites to zoological institutions and sanctuaries.

Why GibbonFamily?

The case for a global gibbon network

Gibbons are one of the most threatened primate species, with all 20 species facing significant conservation challenges.

Individual research teams accumulate rich, long-term datasets, but these rarely come together to address broader comparative questions: HHow do socioecological factors shape pair-bond group dynamics and individual's cognitive ability? How can these research contribute to conservation?

GibbonFamily exists to change this. We believe a connected gibbon research community can achieve collective intelligence on better understanding of gibbons.

🌿

Share knowledge

Create a platform for sharing field knowledge, challenges, and practical experience across gibbon field sites and captive settings.

📊

Collaborative analyses

Explore voluntary data sharing for meta-analyses and comparative studies across gibbon species, subspecies, and populations.

🧠

Elevate gibbon science

Raise the profile of gibbon cognition research in wild and captive contexts, drawing attention to gibbons as important study species for ape intelligence research.

🤝

Build community

Strengthen the gibbon research community and create opportunities for collaborative grants and research initiatives.

Core Principles

How we operate

GibbonFamily is built on principles of openness, equity, and researcher autonomy. Participation is always voluntary, and all members retain full ownership of their data.

  • Voluntary participation: Researchers join, share, and collaborate entirely on their own terms.
  • Equitable credit: All contributors to shared datasets and collaborative analyses receive appropriate recognition and authorship based on discussion.
  • Open science: We encourage transparent methods, pre-registration, and open publication wherever possible.
  • Inclusive: GibbonFamily welcomes researchers at all career stages, from all countries and institutional settings.
  • Conservation-linked: Our research aims support the conservation of gibbons and their habitats.
